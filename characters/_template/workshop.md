## 2. Exploration

Use only the questions that matter for this concept. Add, remove, or reorder them freely.

### Play experience

- What should this character regularly get to do that feels satisfying?
- What should their turns or scenes feel like: decisive, clever, dangerous, protective, strange, social, or something else?
- How much mechanical complexity and resource tracking sounds enjoyable?

### Story and personality

- What do they want, and what makes that difficult?
- What contradiction makes them more than their archetype?
- What belief, bond, fear, or flaw is likely to create useful choices at the table?
- Why would they stay with an adventuring party when things become costly?

### World connection

- What parts of the campaign premise or setting should they naturally engage with?
- Which relationships, factions, duties, debts, or mysteries already pull on them?
- What should remain open for the GM and play to define?

### Mechanical expression

- Which parts of the fantasy must be represented mechanically rather than only through flavor?
- Is there a preferred role, signature action, class, species, background, feat, item, or spell?
- What mechanics would undermine the desired fantasy even if they are powerful?
