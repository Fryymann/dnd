### Level-2 magic item plans

Bench knows four plans and can maintain two replicated items at level 3.

Recommended working four based on the options available in Ian's D&D Beyond builder:

1. `Alchemy Jug` — configured as the integrated fuel plant. Working fuel rate: its full daily output of 4 gallons of beer equals 1 Fuel Unit, pending Marc.
2. `Shield, +1` — raises the shield's total contribution to AC by 1 without requiring attunement; target AC becomes 20 with scale mail and Integrated Protection.
3. `Wand of Secrets` — three charges, regaining 1d3 daily; detects and points toward the nearest secret door or trap within 60 feet. It can also serve as Bench's Artificer focus because it is a replicated Wand.
4. `Bag of Holding` — integrated map archive, tool locker, loot compartment, and repository for stolen spoons.

Default tomb pair: `Shield, +1` and `Wand of Secrets`. Use `Alchemy Jug` on refueling days and `Bag of Holding` when storage matters. Strong alternatives include `Goggles of Night`, `Manifold Tool`, `Wand of Magic Detection`, `Wand of the War Mage, +1`, `Sending Stones`, and `Rope of Climbing`.

The builder also exposes `Spellwrought Tattoo, 1st level`. This is unusually powerful because it can create a consumable first-level spell outside Bench's normal preparation, and some interpretations enable permanent or repeatable exploits. Do not make it a core plan unless Marc explicitly approves how the plan chooses its contained spell and what happens to persistent effects.

## Tactics and play patterns

### Default turn or scene

- Open with `Faerie Fire` when several enemies can be exposed for the party.
- Use `Grease` when non-Concentration control is more valuable or when Dexterity saves look favorable.
- Use Cartographer map effects to preserve targeting, routes, and ally positioning.
- Fall back to `Fire Bolt` while concentration or positioning effects are active.

### Resource-light approach

- Rely on `Fire Bolt`, `Mage Hand`, tools, Lucky, and the currently replicated items.
- Preserve spell slots when ordinary surveying, Investigation, or map-linked coordination can solve the problem.

### High-impact approach

- Establish `Faerie Fire`, distribute or exploit Cartographer maps, and spend slots on control, rescue, or emergency healing.
- Use `Feather Fall` as the emergency reaction; Bench's map says the route includes “down,” not “terminal velocity.”

### Weak matchups and fallback plan

- High-Dexterity enemies can resist both `Faerie Fire` and `Grease`; switch to direct damage, tools, and ally support.
- Fire resistance weakens `Fire Bolt`; prepare or select an alternate damage option when the campaign makes this common.
- Concentration pressure makes the level-4 feat decision important.

### Team synergies

- Attack-heavy allies benefit strongly from targets exposed by `Faerie Fire`.
- Cartographer map holders can coordinate effects through obstructed positions within the feature's range.
- Bench protects his “assets” with emergency healing and movement effects while denying any emotional motive.
