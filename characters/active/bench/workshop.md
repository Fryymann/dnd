# Character workshop

## 1. Seed

Capture the raw idea before improving it.

- Initial prompt or inspiration: Build an Artificer Cartographer using the revised Warforged species.
- Desired fantasy: A constructed magical cartographer whose body, tools, and subclass abilities feel designed around exploration and spatial manipulation.
- Desired emotional tone: Comedic, boastful, opportunistic, and irreverent with a real emotional core and denied loyalty.
- Memorable visual, behavior, or scene: A Warforged consulting or projecting a map that changes as reality, routes, or tactical positions change.
- Known constraints: Use the released versions from *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer*. Campaign setting and starting level are not yet known.

## 4. Chosen concept

- Character premise: A Warforged manufactured as a Cartographic Reproduction Unit who copied maps and tomb records, began correcting them without authorization, and developed the same reproduction logic into Artificer magic-item plans and reality-editing cartography.
- Central tension: He genuinely cares little for most people or principles and loudly acts for personal advantage, but becomes fiercely loyal to a small chosen crew and repeatedly creates maps, routes, and escape plans that keep those friends together.
- Party contribution: Reconnaissance, spatial manipulation, clever routes, broad Artificer utility, and comic confidence; exact combat emphasis remains open.
- Signature behavior or imagery: Integrated measuring tools, compulsive map correction, exaggerated professional pride, routes that suspiciously prioritize treasure and taverns, and private records of places important to companions.
- Mechanical promise: Cartographer abilities turn “I know a shortcut” into reality-altering navigation rather than a recurring joke with no game expression.
- Narrative promise: A being made to reproduce someone else's records becomes someone who corrects maps, redraws routes, and refuses to be treated as interchangeable equipment.

## 6. Conversation log

Append a section for each substantial design conversation.

### 2026-07-25 — Bender-inspired self-made cartographer

- **Starting question:** Can the character borrow Bender's personality and a mundane-function origin without copying his story?
- **Ian's answer or direction:** Base the personality on Bender more than the backstory. Potentially have the Warforged designed for a mundane function before independently breaking into cartography, analogous to moving beyond a repetitive industrial purpose.
- **What it implies:** Build a boastful, opportunistic comic personality with genuine attachment beneath denial. His copying machinery should be materially visible and should evolve directly into Artificer replication and Cartographer magic.
- **Options considered:** Purpose-built military cartographer, postwar explorer, survey datum unit, and cartographic reproduction unit attached to tomb expeditions.
- **Decision or current lean:** Mulhorandi Tomb Raider and mundane map copier turned unauthorized editor, replicator, and self-made Cartographer.
- **Still open:** Maker, owner, expedition organization, departure incident, exact copying apparatus, and route into Marc's setting.
