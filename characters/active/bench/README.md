# Bench — Warforged Cartographer

> A constructed explorer whose maps do more than describe the world: they help him determine how people move through it.

## Status

- Stage: exploration
- Codename: Bench, shortened from “Workbench,” the nickname used when he functioned as a cartographic copying bench
- Ruleset: Revised Artificer and Warforged from *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer*
- Campaign: prospective campaign; setting undecided
- Player/NPC: player character
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

## Intent snapshot

- **I want to feel:** Like Bender reinterpreted as a Warforged Cartographer: boastful, degenerate, greedy, funny, and fiercely loyal to a very small circle.
- **At the table I want to:** Enter drinking competitions, make robot jokes, pinch trivial objects from consenting friends for comedy, and use the new Cartographer and Warforged mechanics creatively; exact combat role remains open.
- **The image or idea that started this:** A Warforged Artificer who maps unknown places and manipulates routes, position, and terrain through crafted charts.
- **Must keep:** Revised Warforged; revised Artificer; Cartographer subclass; cartography as identity rather than decorative proficiency.
- **Want to avoid:** Reducing the character to a generic robot, GPS joke, ordinary Artificer carrying a map, direct Bender imitation, or party-hostile comic relief.

## Current direction

Develop Bench, a Warforged originally manufactured as a Cartographic Reproduction Unit: a mundane copying machine for maps, tomb plans, architectural drawings, inscriptions, and expedition records. Workers called him “Workbench” because originals and blank sheets were laid across his integrated copying surface. He learned to understand, correct, and eventually create the documents he was built only to duplicate. His Mulhorandi Tomb Raider background connects that work to tomb expeditions, while Replicate Magic Item extends his original copying function from documents into magic objects. His personality draws from Bender's grandiosity, flexible ethics, drinking, laziness, insults, petty theft, and deeply denied loyalty. Bench is alcohol-fueled and carries an integrated brewing apparatus; exact fuel rules remain Marc's final call.
