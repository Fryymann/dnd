# Alcohol-fueled core — proposal for Marc

> Status: homebrew proposal. This is not active until Marc approves it.

## Design goal

Bench is powered by alcohol. He must regularly refuel or his constructed body begins shutting down, represented by Exhaustion. The mechanic should create drinking jokes, supply decisions, brewing scenes, and occasional complications without turning the campaign into daily fuel accounting.

The revised Warforged species already provides its sleep and rest behavior. Bench doesn't need to sleep, magic can't put him to sleep, and he can finish a Long Rest in six hours spent inactive and motionless. The alcohol requirement is therefore a voluntary drawback, not the price paid for Sentry's Rest.

## Recommended rule

### Alcoholic Core

Bench has an internal fuel reserve with a maximum of 3 Fuel Units.

- Every 24 hours, his core consumes 1 Fuel Unit.
- He can refill the reserve by consuming suitable alcoholic drink. Marc determines equivalencies when the exact drink matters; ordinary access to a tavern or merchant should make one unit inexpensive.
- If the reserve is empty when the core would consume a unit, Bench gains 1 level of Exhaustion.
- He gains another level of Exhaustion after each additional 24 hours without fuel.
- Refueling stops further Exhaustion but doesn't immediately remove levels already gained. Those recover through the normal rules, including Long Rests.
- Fuel provides no combat bonus, healing, Temporary Hit Points, spell slots, or other numerical benefit.

The three-unit reserve gives the party time to react to a supply problem while preserving the fiction that alcohol is necessary.

## Fuel versus intoxication

Fuel and drunkenness are separate questions.

- Alcohol used to fill an empty reserve powers the core.
- Alcohol consumed beyond ordinary fuel needs can make Bench intoxicated if Marc uses intoxication rules.
- Bench can still enter drinking competitions, overconsume, cheat, or make terrible decisions.
- The fuel trait doesn't automatically grant immunity to intoxication or permission to ignore whatever poison and condition rules Marc uses.

A regulator or bypass switch can explain why he sometimes drinks enormous quantities without impairment and sometimes becomes obviously drunk. Whether he can deliberately manipulate that switch during a competition is a character joke first and a GM ruling second.

## Integrated brewer's supplies

Bench has brewer's supplies incorporated into his torso as a compact alchemical fermentation and distillation assembly.

### Official Artificer representation

At Artificer level 2, Bench learns the `Alchemy Jug` magic-item plan. The 2024 `Alchemy Jug` can produce beer, so a replicated jug can be integrated into his torso as the magical high-output mode of the brewery.

- The `Alchemy Jug` occupies one of Bench's two active replicated-item slots at level 3.
- Working recommendation: 1 Fuel Unit equals the jug's full daily beer output—4 gallons—or an equivalent quantity of stronger alcohol. Marc makes the final equivalency ruling.
- At that rate, the jug sustains Bench but doesn't refill a depleted multi-day reserve overnight; purchased or separately brewed alcohol is needed to rebuild the buffer quickly.
- Devoting an active item to self-sufficient fuel is the mechanical opportunity cost; Bench could use that slot for another replicated item instead.
- If the jug isn't currently replicated, Bench falls back to the slower ingredient-based brewer below or purchased alcohol.

### Mundane/alchemical fallback

Recommended implementation:

- Bench gains proficiency with Brewer's Supplies through a legal class, background, or tool choice rather than receiving a free proficiency from this homebrew.
- The integrated apparatus functions as Brewer's Supplies and occupies part of his body, but it still requires suitable ingredients, water, and time.
- If loaded with suitable ingredients, it slowly produces 1 Fuel Unit over 24 hours.
- The apparatus can continue working while Bench travels or enters Sentry's Rest, provided it was loaded beforehand and isn't damaged or deliberately shut down.
- It can't produce more while the 3-unit reserve is full.
- Fuel made this way is harsh, unstable, and tailored to Bench's core. It has negligible resale value and shouldn't become an infinite-money machine.
- Foraged ingredients require Marc to agree that appropriate sugars, starches, fruit, grain, or similar fermentable materials are available. Purchased ingredients should be cheap but not literally free.

The built-in brewer is a supply safety valve and roleplaying engine, not a source of commercial-scale alcohol.

## Standby mode

Bench's standby mode uses the revised Warforged Sentry's Rest rules rather than replacing them:

- He spends six hours inactive and motionless.
- He appears inert.
- He does not sleep and can't be put to sleep by magic.
- He completes a Long Rest as allowed by the species.
- Whether he can meaningfully perform watch duties beyond perceiving his surroundings must follow the exact species wording and Marc's ruling; standby should not grant extra actions during a Long Rest.

The integrated brewer may process a loaded batch automatically during standby because Bench isn't actively operating it.

## Softer alternative

If Marc thinks automatic Exhaustion after a single missed day is too punishing:

- The first 24 hours with an empty reserve produce obvious sputtering, smoke, slowed speech, and warning indicators but no mechanical penalty.
- Exhaustion begins after 48 hours empty and increases every 24 hours thereafter.

This retains the fuel premise while making an accidental bookkeeping miss less costly.

## Harder alternative

If the group wants fuel scarcity to matter strongly:

- Reduce the reserve to 2 Fuel Units.
- Brewing still creates only 1 unit per 24 hours.
- Unusual environments may make suitable ingredients difficult to forage.

Do not add escalating combat penalties beyond the normal Exhaustion rules; doubling the punishment creates unnecessary bookkeeping.

## Questions for Marc

1. Is Alcoholic Core acceptable as a voluntary Warforged drawback?
2. Does Exhaustion begin immediately when a daily unit is missed, or after a 24-hour grace period?
3. What amount of common alcohol counts as 1 Fuel Unit?
4. May the integrated brewer turn suitable purchased or foraged ingredients into 1 Fuel Unit per 24 hours?
5. Brewer's Supplies comes from Bench's Artificer artisan-tool choice; confirm that the integrated apparatus can use that proficiency normally.
6. How does intoxication work in the campaign, especially with Warforged poison-related traits?
7. During Sentry's Rest, what can Bench perceive or do while remaining inactive and motionless?
