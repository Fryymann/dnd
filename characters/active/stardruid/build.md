## Spells or selectable features

Group choices by purpose rather than copying full rules text.

### Level 1 departure repertoire — working plan

**Cantrips**

- Mending — learned with his carpenter/tinkerer father by restoring the line geometry of damaged objects.
- Starry Wisp — developed through family stargazing; his first reliable projection of cosmic energy and basic ranged offense.

Circle of Stars is expected to grant Guidance at level 3, so Guidance is deliberately not selected as a level-1 cantrip. Thorn Whip remains a strong future option if Ian wants more tactical line manipulation.

**Four prepared level-1 spells**

- Cure Wounds — close, deliberate repair of a damaged living lattice; developed largely through his mother's healing knowledge.
- Healing Word — sending a corrective impulse along an existing cosmic connection; his ranged emergency technique.
- Goodberry — applying his mother's herbalism to store stabilizing cosmic energy in living material.
- Faerie Fire — making normally invisible line relationships luminous so allies can exploit them.

**Always prepared from Druidic**

- Speak with Animals — interpreted as communication through the cosmic connections shared by living creatures.

### Human feat — Healer

Healer is granted by Human Versatile. It makes healer's-kit treatment mechanically meaningful and improves the reliability of healing rolls. This is the only feat locked in at level 1.

### Deferred feat candidate — Magic Initiate (Wizard)

Magic Initiate is pushed to level 4 or later and is not currently part of the build. If selected, the working package remains Mage Hand, Light, and Shield using Wisdom, but it must be compared against War Caster and increasing Wisdom before being finalized.

### Level 3 campaign-start direction

- Guidance — expected from Circle of Stars' Star Map; momentarily reveals the most favorable line through a task.
- Guiding Bolt — expected from Star Map, subject to 2024 compatibility ruling; a forceful luminous line that leaves a target exposed.
- Aid — a key level-2 defensive enhancement that increases the resilience of multiple allies without Concentration.
- Lesser Restoration — reinforces his role as a genuine caregiver rather than only an HP restorer.
- Enhance Ability — optional daily preparation when ally enhancement matters more than condition removal.

The 2024 Druid prepares six level 1+ spells at level 3 and can change the list after a Long Rest. A likely default is Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Goodberry, Faerie Fire, Aid, and either Lesser Restoration or Enhance Ability, with other Druid spells prepared for specific voyages or adventuring days.
