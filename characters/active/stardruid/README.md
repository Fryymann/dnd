# StarDruid — working concept

> A blue-collar mathematical genius who can see and manipulate cosmic lines beyond the Weave, using their geometry to preserve life and reshape a battlefield.

## Status

- Stage: exploration
- Codename: StarDruid
- Ruleset: D&D 5.5e, exact source policy undecided
- Campaign: prospective new campaign
- Player/NPC: player character
- Last reviewed: 2026-07-25

## Intent snapshot

- **I want to feel:** Like the party's dedicated caregiver and survival specialist, expressed through an unusual Druid rather than a conventional divine medic.
- **At the table I want to:** Keep damage-focused friends alive through strong healing, buffs, and defense, while still contributing through durability or respectable damage.
- **The image or idea that started this:** An off-brand healer built from Druid features, species traits, and creative feat combinations.
- **Must keep:** Healing and care as primary themes; meaningful ally enhancement or protection; feats and species as active parts of the build; a distinct identity from familiar cleric and paladin experiences.
- **Want to avoid:** Playing another paladin; defaulting to a traditional cleric; making beast-form combat the character's defining feature; being helpless whenever nobody currently needs healing.

## Current direction

Develop a 25-year-old human male Circle of Stars Druid whose central identity is healer, caregiver, sailor/rigger, Linewright, and intuitive mathematical genius. He is not religious, but his direct perception of cosmic connections has made him deeply and privately spiritual. Raised by an herbalist mother and a brilliant carpenter/tinkerer father in a small village beside a forested lake, he left home at fifteen and spent the next decade at sea while returning home several times most years. He independently developed his Linewright practice without meeting another practitioner. Starry Form represents stable cosmic-line configurations for healing, concentration, or directed damage. The campaign can begin wherever Marc's premise places him.
