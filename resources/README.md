# Shared resources

Store reusable material that is not owned by one character or campaign here, such as:

- house-rule proposals and accepted house rules;
- setting-neutral tables and prompts;
- reusable encounter or character-design tools;
- source indexes and rules references;
- shared templates that do not belong in a specific project.

Prefer links, citations, and original summaries over copied rulebook text. Keep campaign-specific rulings with their campaign unless they have become a general table convention.
